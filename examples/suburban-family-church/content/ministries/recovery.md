Recovery at Oakbridge is a Celebrate Recovery group — Christ-centered, 12-step-based, open to anyone affected by addiction, compulsive behavior, or relational hurt. That includes spouses, parents, and friends of someone in recovery.

Confidentiality is the most important rule. What's said in small group stays in small group.

We've been running Thursday nights since 2015. Most weeks we have around 40 people, with small groups by gender and by topic (chemical addiction, food, relationships, codependency, anger).

If this is the first time you've considered coming, just show up. You don't have to talk. You don't have to introduce yourself. Sit in the back. Have coffee. Stay or leave whenever. There are no expectations.
