Young Adults at Oakbridge is for everyone in their 20s navigating the giant decisions of the decade — career, relationships, calling, identity.

Tuesday nights are the anchor. We worship together, hear teaching, then break into smaller groups for discussion. Most weeks we end up at someone's apartment for dessert afterward.

We also do monthly socials (everything from sunset hikes to apartment game nights to fancy dinner club) and an annual fall retreat where most of the actual life-changing conversations happen.
