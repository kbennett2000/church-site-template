The middle-school and high-school years are when faith goes from inherited to owned — or doesn't. Oakbridge Youth is designed for that transition.

We invest in the same students across seven years. Most of our 12th-graders have known their small-group leaders since 6th grade. That continuity is the engine.

### Middle School (6th–8th)

Sunday class at 9 and 11. Wednesday small groups at 6:30. Friday Night Live at 7. Annual summer camp.

### High School (9th–12th)

Sunday class at 9 and 11. Wednesday small groups at 6:30. Friday Night Live at 7. Annual summer camp + annual mission trip.

### Parents

We run a parents' meeting once a quarter — covering what we're studying, what's coming up, and how to talk with your teenager about the topics we're hitting. Parents-of-teens dinner every September.
