  ## Where faith begins

  Faith starts not with our reach toward God, but with God's reach toward us. Hebrews 11 opens a long gallery of ordinary people whose lives turned on one thing: they trusted the God who had spoken.

  **Three things to notice in the text:**

  - Faith is **confidence** in what we hope for (v. 1) — not a vague optimism, but a settled trust grounded in God's character.
  - Faith is **conviction** about what we cannot see (v. 1) — a steady "knowing" even when the evidence is hidden from view.
  - Faith is what *pleases* God (v. 6) — and it always begins with believing two things: that he is, and that he rewards those who seek him.

  ### Take it home this week

  1. Where is God asking you to take one more step of trust?
  2. What "unseen" promise are you tempted to stop believing?
  3. Who in your life is watching how you walk with God right now?
