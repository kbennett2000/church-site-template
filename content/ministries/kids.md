The Kids Ministry exists to help children take their first steps in following Jesus. Most weeks our team teaches a Bible story tied to what the adults are studying upstairs, so families can talk about the same ideas at home over the rest of the week.

We offer programming during the Sunday service for newborn through 5th grade. Many small churches also run a midweek program (like Awana or a kids' club) and a summer Vacation Bible School — both are great next steps once your Sunday morning team is established.
