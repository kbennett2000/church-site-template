Recovery is one of the most quietly powerful ministries a small church can offer. It welcomes people who often feel like they don't fit anywhere — both those fighting addiction or compulsive behavior, and the spouses, parents, and friends walking with them.

Many churches use an existing program (Celebrate Recovery is the most common one) or a locally-built curriculum. The format is usually a brief teaching followed by small-group support time. Confidentiality is the most important rule.
