Women's ministry at Oakbridge is organized around the reality that women in different life stages need different things. We try to make showing up easy.

### Weekly studies

Three Bible studies — Tuesday morning, Wednesday evening, Thursday morning (Moms' Coffee track). Pick whichever fits. Free childcare in every morning slot.

### Mentor program

Each fall we pair women who want a mentor with women willing to mentor. The commitment is light: meet once a month for a year, talk about whatever needs talking about.

### Retreat

A two-day women's retreat each spring, an hour from the church. The single most-mentioned thing in our annual women's ministry survey, every year.
