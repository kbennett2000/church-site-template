## What changed and why

This is the meat of the case study. Pick 4–8 specific improvements over what you had before. For each, write a short paragraph (2–4 sentences) explaining what changed and why it mattered.

Examples to consider:

- Mobile experience
- Sermon archive
- Calendar / events
- Visitor funnel
- Editorial workflow
- Specific ministry pages
- Cost

Don't write all of them — pick the 4–8 that are most distinctive for your church.

---

## What was harder than expected

Be honest. Every adopting church runs into something that took longer than the 30-minute setup estimate. Sharing it helps the next church plan better.

Examples:

- "Photo collection took two months because we couldn't get everyone in the same place."
- "Our denomination's statement of faith is 14 paragraphs; the template's 8-statement structure didn't quite fit."
- "We had to find a new domain registrar because our old one didn't let us add the Vercel DNS records."

Three to five short bullets is plenty.
