Our missions ministry is small and deep rather than wide. We support two missionary families — one in West Africa, one in the Czech Republic — both of whom have visited the church and whom several of our members have visited overseas.

The annual missions offering happens the first Sunday of Advent. Outside of that, you can support either family through the church office.
