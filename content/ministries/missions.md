The Missions ministry exists to remind us that the good news of Jesus is for everyone — our next-door neighbors, our city, and people across the world.

Most small churches build their missions strategy around three or four key partnerships: a missionary family or two they support financially and stay in close touch with, one or two local nonprofits where the congregation regularly serves, and an annual short-term trip.
