The men's breakfast is the quiet center of the church for a lot of our guys. It's been running since the early 90s. The format hasn't changed much: somebody cooks, somebody else gives a short message, and everybody eats and talks until they have to go.

If you've never been, just show up. Park out back. The smell of bacon will tell you you're in the right place.
