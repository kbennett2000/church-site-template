Men's ministry at Oakbridge runs on the principle that men show up when their friends show up. So we built four entry points and let guys pick what fits.

### Wednesday Discipleship Night

Same small group of 6–8 men, every Wednesday at 6:30. Picked in September, runs through May. The group works through a book together and prays for each other's actual lives.

### Saturday F3

Free, open to anyone (neighbors who aren't part of the church are welcome). Bodyweight workouts in the parking lot, sometimes a ruck walk or run. Ends with a short prayer.

### Mentor program

Each fall, older men sign up to mentor younger men. Light commitment — coffee once a month, talk about whatever needs talking about. Most pairs renew year after year.

### Annual retreat

Two days, an hour from the church, every October. Worship, teaching, and time to talk through what God is doing.
