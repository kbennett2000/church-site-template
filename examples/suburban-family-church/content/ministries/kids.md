Oakbridge Kids is designed for families with kids at every stage from infancy through 5th grade. We run four age-tiered classrooms during each of our two Sunday services, plus Kids' Wednesdays through the school year, plus a five-day VBS each summer.

Each age tier has its own classroom, its own curriculum, and a dedicated lead teacher who serves the same group all year — so your kid sees a familiar face week after week.

The whole program follows a year-long teaching plan that maps to what's being taught from the pulpit, with take-home guides for parents to keep the conversation going during the week.

### Quick reference

- **Nursery (0–2):** Cribs, rockers, age-appropriate toys. Background-checked volunteers only.
- **Preschool (3–PreK):** Bible story, songs, crafts, snack.
- **Lower Elementary (K–2):** Bible lesson, small-group conversation, games.
- **Upper Elementary (3–5):** Bible lesson, deeper discussion, leadership opportunities.

### Annual events

- **Easter egg hunt** — Saturday before Palm Sunday.
- **Summer VBS** — five-day program every June.
- **Fall Family Fest** — last Saturday in October. Bring a friend.
- **Christmas Eve Kids' Service** — 4:00 PM, family-friendly.
