# Our Story

Oakbridge Community Church was planted in 2003 with about twelve families meeting in the cafeteria of Willow Creek Elementary. Today we're a congregation of around 400 — still family-focused, still committed to making the gospel accessible to people at every life stage.

We exist to help families *of every age* follow Jesus together. That shapes everything from our two Sunday service styles (Traditional at 9 and Contemporary at 11) to our age-tiered kids' programming, to the men's and women's discipleship tracks that run on the same weeknight so couples can come together.

**Two Sunday services. Nursery through high school programming in both. Coffee bar open at 8:30.**

Whether you're brand new to faith, returning after a long time away, or you've been following Jesus since you were a kid — you'll find a place here.
