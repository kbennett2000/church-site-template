The women's Bible study has been running on and off for thirty years and has shrunk and grown as the church has. We're currently about a dozen women, mostly retirees and a couple of moms whose kids are at school. New faces welcome any week.

We've spent the last two years working through the gospel of Luke. Whatever we start next, we'll start it together in September.
