Our Sunday school is small and simple. The kids stay in the service for the singing, then head down the hall for their own class while the adults hear the message. They come back up for the final hymn and benediction.

If you have a child who'd rather stay with you the whole time, that's fine — there are no rules. We're glad you're here.
