Oakbridge's missions strategy is built on five long-term missionary partnerships and three local nonprofit partnerships. We send money, we send people, and we keep relationships warm year after year.

### Long-term partnerships

- A church plant in Kenya — supported since 2008.
- A Bible translation team in Papua New Guinea.
- A campus ministry at a state university 30 minutes away.
- An anti-trafficking ministry in Southeast Asia.
- An indigenous-led church planter in Mexico.

### Local partnerships

- A local food pantry — we staff every Tuesday afternoon.
- A pregnancy resource center — we run the donation drive twice a year.
- A refugee resettlement nonprofit — we sponsor 2–3 families per year.

### Short-term trips

- **High school mission trip** — third week of July.
- **Adults short-term trip** — first week of October.
- **College spring break trip** — spring break week.

Applications for each open three months in advance.
