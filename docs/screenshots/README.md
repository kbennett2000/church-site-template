# Screenshots — a visual tour

A walk through every page of the template and the browser-based editor, shown with a fully populated example church, **Cedar Ridge Church**.

🌐 **[Explore the live demo →](https://church-site-template-psi.vercel.app)**

> *The example photos and faces here are illustrative. Your copy ships with generic placeholder content — tasteful gradient images and initials avatars — that you swap for your own.*

---

## The website

### Homepage

A warm hero leads with your service times and a "Plan Your Visit" button — the first thing a newcomer needs — followed by this week's events, the latest sermon, and your ministries.

![Homepage](app/homepage-full.png)

### Plan a Visit

Answers the questions a first-time visitor actually has: where to park, what to wear, what to expect Sunday morning, and where the kids go — with a short "let us know you're coming" form.

![Plan a Visit page](app/visit.png)

### Ministries

One card per ministry on the index, each opening to its own page with a leader, meeting times, and a "what to expect" list.

![Ministries index](app/ministries.png)

![A ministry page](app/ministry-kids.png)

### Sermons

A searchable archive filterable by series, speaker, scripture, and book of the Bible — with YouTube embeds, a podcast feed, and per-sermon notes.

![Sermon archive](app/watch.png)

![A single sermon with video and notes](app/watch-sermon.png)

### Calendar

Recurring weekly gatherings and one-off events, with `.ics` export so anyone can add them to their own calendar.

![Events calendar](app/calendar.png)

### About & Beliefs

Your story and the people who lead the church, plus a plain-language statement of what you believe.

![About page](app/about.png)

![What we believe](app/beliefs.png)

### Giving

Multiple ways to give (online, text-to-give, mail, in person) and a tax-deductible FAQ.

![Giving page](app/give.png)

## Editing in the browser (TinaCMS)

Staff sign in with Google and edit content visually — no code, no Git. Saving commits the change and the site rebuilds and goes live on its own in a couple of minutes.

### The dashboard

Every type of content — sermons, ministries, staff, events, pages, site settings — lives in the sidebar.

![TinaCMS dashboard](editor/getting-started-dashboard.png)

Pick a collection to see its entries, then fill in a friendly form. (Scripture references are auto-formatted; the book dropdown powers the archive's filters.)
