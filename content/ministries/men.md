Most men don't sign up for things — they show up because a friend invited them. That's why the Men's Ministry is built around two open-door gatherings: a monthly breakfast and a weekly early-morning study.

Many small churches also organize a service day twice a year (helping a widow with yard work, fixing something at a local nonprofit) and an annual retreat for deeper friendship and teaching.
