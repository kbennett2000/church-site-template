The young adult years are a season of big questions: career, marriage, calling, identity, and how faith fits into all of it. Young Adults is a space to ask those questions out loud with people in the same stage of life.

A weekly Bible study is the anchor; many churches add monthly social events, an annual retreat, and intentional pairing with older mentors in the congregation.
