# Our Story

Prairie Community Church has been gathering on the same corner of County Road 9 for over forty years. We're a small church on purpose — about sixty people on a typical Sunday — and that's how we like it. Everyone knows everyone. The kids run between the pews and nobody minds. Newcomers get hugged before they get questioned.

We don't have a youth band or a video screen or a coffee bar. What we have is a Bible, a piano, a stack of folding chairs in the basement for the potluck, and a congregation that's been walking with Jesus together for decades.

If you're new in town — or just curious — come on Sunday. We'll save you a seat. Stay for coffee. Stay for the potluck if it's the second Sunday. Either way, you're welcome.

**Sundays at 9:00 AM. 412 County Road 9, Prairieville.**
